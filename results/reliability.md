# Recording-level analysis

Main pipeline = ensemble + AF rule + fusion gate. Generated by `analysis.py`.

## 1. Fusion gate

F predictions are dropped in recordings where fewer than 1% of beats are predicted V (a fusion beat needs a ventricular beat). The threshold is fixed in advance.

Check on DS1 (out-of-fold logistic regression): macro-F1 0.493 → 0.493, F Se 48.5% → 48.5%, F +P 4.5% → 5.7%.

## 2. Reliability flag

Consistency = median correlation between each beat and the patient's median beat (lead A). A recording is flagged when consistency < **0.975** (chosen on DS1 patients only, Youden's J for accuracy < 90%). No labels and no model are needed to compute it.

| Test set | Recordings | Flagged | Failing (acc < 90%) | Failing and flagged | Flagged but fine | Accuracy, trusted | Accuracy, flagged | AUROC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DS2 | 22 | 8 | 10 | 5 | 3 | 92.0% | 87.1% | 0.80 |
| SVDB | 78 | 50 | 50 | 45 | 5 | 94.1% | 62.0% | 0.94 |
| INCART | 75 | 51 | 54 | 44 | 7 | 91.9% | 65.6% | 0.86 |
