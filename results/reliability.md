## 3. Reliability warning and its causes

- **Unstable beats** (the warning): share of on-time beats that match none of the patient's own beat patterns (correlation < 0.9). Warning above **6%**.
- **Frequent early beats** (information): share of beats arriving > 15% early. Shown above **5%**.
- **Several beat patterns** (information): the patient switches between beat shapes.
- For comparison, the first version of the flag: median correlation with a single median beat < **0.975**.

A recording "fails" when its beat accuracy is below 90%. No labels and no model are needed to compute any signal.
